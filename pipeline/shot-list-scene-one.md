# Shot List — Scene One

Mountain treehouse. Dan DaCaveman hanging by toenails, rope dangling unused. Tom watching from below.

## Shot 1 — Establishing wide
Wide shot: mountain face, treehouse embedded in a tree, tall windows glowing, chandeliers visible inside. Dan's silhouette hanging off the side, rope dangling beside him.

## Shot 2 — Dan's hands/feet detail
Close-up: razor-sharp ancient toenails embedded in the tree bark, fingers gripping. Rope hangs unused nearby.

## Shot 3 — Interior through window
Through the tall window: vaulted ceilings, chandeliers, clean wooden floors, luxurious couches, large flat-screen TV mounted on the wall.

## Shot 4 — Tom's reaction
Tom standing below, staring up at his father, concerned.

## Shot 5 — Dialogue
Two-shot: Tom calls up, "Come on pop. At least hook the rope to your carabiner, for the allusion of safety." Dan looks down, "You being serious right now?"

## Prompt notes

- Character consistency: Dan = muscular caveman, five foot five, thick ancient nails, beard and goatee, cloth around waist. Tom = younger caveman, similar build.
- Tone: deadpan comedy. Dan treats the danger as normal.
- Keep the rope visually present but unused — the joke is the illusion of safety.
