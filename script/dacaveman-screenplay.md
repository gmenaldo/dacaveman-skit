# DaCaveman

Written by Alfred Gause. Format only. No spoken line is rewritten. The one "Dave" in the original is Tom.

## Scene 1 — Mountain treehouse

<p align="right">FADE IN:</p>

**EXT. MOUNTAIN — TREEHOUSE — DAY**

A house is built into a tree on the side of a mountain. Tall windows open onto the living room: vaulted ceilings, chandeliers, clean wooden floors, luxurious couches, and a large flat-screen television.

**Dan DaCaveman** hangs off the side of the house. He is five foot five, strong and chiseled, with a superior muscular build, bright eyes in deep sockets, a nice face, thick eyebrows, a beard and goatee, and sharp teeth. His legs and thighs are heavily muscled. A cloth covers his waist, his buttocks, and his genitals. His fingernails and toenails are super long, razor sharp, thick, and ancient.

A rope dangles beside him. It looks as if the rope is holding him. It is not. His toenails are embedded in the tree. He is putting the finishing touches on the house.

**Tom DaCaveman** stands below and stares up at his father.

**Tom**

> Come on pop. At least hook the rope to your carabiner, for the allusion of safety.

**Dan**

> You being serious right now?

## Scene 2 — The news and the birthday call

<p align="right">DISSOLVE TO:</p>

**INT. DAN'S HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — DAY**

Dan sits in his chair, in pajamas, listening to the news. Past his right shoulder: a large fireplace, an old medieval crock pot, and a pig on a rotisserie.

**News Announcer (on TV)**

> This just in, the illegal Alien Ban has been lifted. All aliens in the U.S. and across the globe are considered legal. You are all free to—

He pauses.

> be you!

**MONTAGE — ON THE TELEVISION**

El Salvadorian immigrants dance.

Jamaican immigrants dance.

Chinese, Ukrainian, and Russian immigrants dance across the world.

**END MONTAGE**

<p align="right">CUT TO:</p>

**INT. TOM'S PLACE — DAY**

Tom sits watching the same broadcast. He blinks, surprised. He calls his father.

**Tom**

> Happy born day pops. What you up to?

**Dan**

> You know me son, sittin around relaxing.

**Tom**

> Let's go out to eat. Got a birthday surprise for you.

**Dan**

> You know I hate going out in public.

**Tom**

> Get dressed. I'm on my way.

## Scene 3 — The ride

<p align="right">CUT TO:</p>

**EXT. DAN'S HOUSE — DAY**

Tom honks. Dan comes out dressed for the winter: heavy coat, hat, boots, gloves, and scarf. Hair grows out of the hat. His beard is long. He gets in. They drive off.

<p align="right">CUT TO:</p>

**INT. CAR — DAY**

**Tom**

> We ain't been out.

**Dan**

> You know I hate going out in public.

**Tom**

> Pop, I wouldn't ask you to go out if it wasn't ok.

**Dan**

> So, you mean, it passed?

**Tom**

> Everyone's excited!

**Dan**

> They have every reason to be.

**Tom**

> To celebrate, how bout a mani-pedi?

**Dan**

> Always wondered about those. What do they do?

**Tom**

> Well, two good looking women work on your feet and hands at the same time while massaging your calf muscles.

**Dan**

> Sounds good. I'm overdue to be pampered. Then, can we eat?

**Tom**

> Just sit back and enjoy the show.

<p align="right">CUT TO:</p>

**EXT. NAIL SALON — DAY**

They arrive at the shop.

## Scene 4 — The nail salon

<p align="right">CUT TO:</p>

**INT. NAIL SALON — CONTINUOUS**

A wide picture window. Raised leather chairs with running foot baths, and the same chairs on the lower level. A display of nail tips. A television shows the Illegal Alien Act decision.

Their claws are covered. Two people in shorts and T-shirts stand behind them. The shop worker wears a blue nurse outfit. Her hair is short, dark, and medium-long.

**Shop Worker**

> Hello. Can I get the name for the appointment?

**Tom**

> Tom, and Dan DaCaveman.

**Shop Worker**

> Have a seat right here.

They sit at the window. People gather outside.

**Dan**

> Wow, son! I feel good.

**Tom**

> Told you. This one of the best feelings in the world. To have women rub and pamper you.

Tom looks at his dad.

**Tom**

> Be comfortable. We don't have to hide anymore. It's the law.

**Dan**

> Everything?

**Tom**

> Everything, pop. Watch me!

Tom breathes and transforms. Sackcloth covers his chest like a tank top and covers him from the waist to his upper thighs. His nails are exposed. People gasp.

Dan breathes and transforms. Sackcloth covers only his waist to his upper thighs. Most of his body is exposed, including his chest hair. People outside run to the glass.

**INSERT — TOM'S HANDS AND FEET**

His fingernails are as long as his fingers. His toenails are prehistorically thick.

**BACK TO SCENE**

**INSERT — DAN'S FEET**

His ancient toenails are in the running water.

**BACK TO SCENE**

**Dan**

> Ah, it's good to finally give my feet some air!

People film them. Tom stares at his father in amazement.

**Dan**

> What's wrong son?

**Tom**

> You could've left some clothing on.

Two guests run out.

**Dan**

> My fir got me covered.

The person beside them throws up a little in their mouth. Two employees come out, see people leaving, and stop in front of them.

**Employee One**

> How are you guys doing ohh my God!

He jumps back.

**Employee Two**

> What the hell am I looking at?

Employee One walks in a circle.

**Employee One**

> We gotta close the shop! We gotta close the shop!

The owner runs out and almost falls.

**Owner**

> Are you freakin kidding me! The ban hasn't even been lifted for twenty-fours yet. I thought you guys only come out at night!

**Dan**

> I know that song! The freaks come out at night! Nope, me and my son here for the free mani-pedi, it's my birthday.

The owner looks at them in disgust and takes deep gulps.

**Owner**

> That's nasty.

He yells through a bullhorn toward the back of the shop.

**Owner**

> Get me a chisel, a sander and a hazmat bucket. Better make it two hazmat buckets, a few face shields and some thick rubber gloves!

**Employee One**

> Naw man! One of y'all gonna have to pay!

**Owner**

> You can't say that!

**Employee One**

> What you mean? We can close the shop for the week on this ticket alone!

The employees work on the nails, uncomfortable. The owner checks in a woman. She sees the cavemen, breathes, and transforms. Sackcloth covers her chest and her waist to the middle of her thighs. Her nails match theirs.

**Cavewoman**

> It's my birthday!

<p align="right">FADE OUT.</p>
